Your data is automatically saved. Every lead you add, every call you book, and every customer you advance is saved to this device the moment you do it. 1
First-Time Setup
Do these two things before anything else
🔑
1
Get your Anthropic API key
Go to console.anthropic.com in any browser. Sign in or create a free account. Click API Keys in the left menu. Click Create Key. Give it a name like "Sales Agent Blueprint." Copy the key — it starts with sk-ant-.
⚠️ Copy it immediately — you can only see it once
2
Paste your API key into the app
Look at the top right of the screen — you will see a dark input field that says "Paste Anthropic API key for AI Agent…" Click it and paste your key. It saves automatically. From this moment on, the AI Agent on screen 2 is fully live and powered by Claude.
✅ The key is saved — you never have to paste it again on this device
3
Clear the sample data when you're ready to use your own
The app opens with sample leads, bookings, and customers already loaded so you can see how everything works. When you are ready to start fresh with your own real data, scroll to the very bottom of the left sidebar and click ↺ Reset Sample Data. This clears everything and gives you a blank slate. You only need to do this once.
📌 Only reset when you're ready — your real leads will replace the samples
2
Dashboard — Your Morning Command Center
Open this first every day
⚡
1
Read the four stat cards at the top
These show your Revenue This Month, Active Leads, Calls Booked, and Deals Closed. They give you a snapshot of your business health in under 10 seconds. If any number looks low, that is your focus for the day.
2
Check the Live Agent Activity feed
The right side of the dashboard shows what your agent has been doing — emails sent, leads scored, calls booked. It updates automatically every 7 seconds with a new simulated activity. Read it like a morning briefing.
3
Review the Sales Funnel card
The left card shows how many leads are moving through each stage — from captured to email open to call booked to closed. If you see a big drop between two stages, that is where your energy belongs today.
4
Use the Quick Action buttons to jump to your most urgent task
The four gold-bordered boxes at the bottom link directly to Ask the AI Agent, Add New Lead, Launch Email Sequence, and Book a Discovery Call. Tap whichever one matches what you need most right now.
3
AI Agent — Your 24/7 Sales Brain
Requires your Anthropic API key — see Step 1 of Setup
🤖
1
Use the prompt chips for instant answers
At the top of the chat window you will see six blue pill-shaped chips. Tap any one to instantly ask that question — Write me a cold email pitch, Handle the "I need to think about it" objection, What should I say on a discovery call?, and more. The agent answers immediately with a full, specific response.
💡 These chips are the fastest way to get help in under 30 seconds
2
Type any question in the chat box
Click the dark input box at the bottom of the chat screen. Type your question — it can be anything about pitching, licensing, objections, pricing, or strategy. Press Enter or tap the gold arrow button to send. The agent responds in a few seconds.
3
Have a full back-and-forth conversation
The agent remembers everything said in the current session. Ask it to rewrite something, go deeper on a topic, or change the tone. Example: "Write me a cold email for a beauty product inventor" → "Now make it shorter" → "Add a P.S. line about the guarantee." It builds on each reply.
4
Clear the chat to start a fresh topic
Tap the Clear Chat button in the top right of the agent screen. This wipes the current conversation so you can start a completely new topic without the old context interfering. Use this between different tasks — for example after writing a pitch email, clear chat before asking for objection scripts.
4
Lead Engine — Capture Every Prospect
Add a lead the moment someone expresses interest
🎯
1
Fill in the Add New Lead form on the left
Enter the person's Full Name and Email — these two fields are required. Add their product type (kitchen tool, beauty gadget, etc.), how they found you, and their urgency level. The urgency field is the most important — it determines their score.
2
Tap "Add & Score Lead" and read the result
The app automatically assigns an A, B, or C score based on urgency. A = Ready to buy or has a patent (book them immediately). B = Actively researching (start nurture sequence). C = Just browsing (add to long-term follow-up).
🏆 Never let an A-lead sit more than 24 hours without a booking attempt
3
Take action on every lead using the buttons
Each lead card has three buttons: ✉ Nurture — starts their email sequence. 📅 Book — opens the Booking screen with their name pre-filled so you can schedule a call immediately. ✕ — removes the lead permanently when they are closed or no longer relevant. Use all three regularly to keep your list clean and active.
4
Read the Lead Scoring Model on the right to understand the system
The card on the right side explains exactly what each score means and what action it triggers. Refer to it any time you are unsure what to do with a new lead.
5
Pipeline CRM — Track Every Deal
Nothing falls through the cracks when every deal has a column
📊
1
Read the five columns left to right — that is your deal journey
Every deal moves from left to right: New Lead → Nurture → Call Booked → Proposal Sent → Closed Won. The green column on the far right is your wins. A deal stuck in the same column for more than 7 days needs a direct action from you.
2
Look at the progress bar on each deal card
Every deal card has a thin colored bar at the bottom — it shows how far along that deal is as a percentage. Gold = early stage. Blue = mid-stage. Green = near close. Use this as a quick visual scan to identify your hottest opportunities without reading every card.
3
Spend 5 minutes here every morning
Scroll through each column. Any name that has not moved in a week — go to the AI Agent and ask: "Write a re-engagement message for [name] who has been in the Nurture stage for 10 days." The agent will write it. You send it. The deal moves.
📌 Your most important daily habit — 5 minutes on the pipeline board
6
Booking — Fill Your Calendar with Hot Calls
Use this any time an A-lead is ready to talk
📅
1
Select a date on the calendar
Only the highlighted dates have available slots — tap one to select it. The selected date appears in gold above the time slots. Days marked as already booked are shown in a darker style and cannot be selected.
2
Choose an available time slot
Tap any white time slot on the right panel — it turns gold when selected. Green slots are already booked and cannot be chosen. Once you select a time, it stays highlighted until you confirm or pick a different one.
3
Enter the lead's name and call type, then tap Confirm Booking
Type the lead's name in the field below the time slots. Choose the call type — Discovery Call (30 min), Strategy Session (60 min), or Deal Review (45 min). Add any context notes you want to remember. Tap 📅 Confirm Booking — the booking saves instantly and appears in the Upcoming Calls list below.
✅ Bookings are saved permanently — they will still be there when you reopen the app
4
Use the Prep Notes button before every call
In the Upcoming Calls section, each booking has a Prep Notes button. Tap it — the app jumps to the AI Agent screen with a prep request already typed for that person. Hit send and the agent gives you a full call preparation guide in seconds.
5
Cancel a booking with the ✕ button
If a call gets cancelled or rescheduled, tap the small ✕ button on the right side of the booking card. It removes it from your list permanently. Then go back to the calendar and book a new time.
7
Email Sequences — Your Automated Nurture System
7 emails already written — ready to paste into your email platform
✉️
1
Read all 7 emails from top to bottom — once
Each email card shows the timing, subject line, preview copy, estimated open rate, and the strategic goal for that email. Read all 7 once so you understand the full arc — from first welcome to final re-engagement. This makes every other part of the app make more sense.
2
Copy the emails into your email platform
These emails are ready to use as written. Go to your email platform — Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Flodesk, or ActiveCampaign — create a new automation sequence, and paste each email in order with the correct timing delays. Day 0 (immediate), Day 2, Day 4, Day 7, Day 10, Day 14, Day 21.
💡 Once set up in your email platform, this sequence runs forever with zero effort from you
3
Watch the estimated open rates as your benchmark
Each email shows an estimated open rate. Email 1 targets 52%. If your real open rate is lower than 10 percentage points below the estimate, ask the AI Agent to rewrite that specific email's subject line. Example: "Rewrite a subject line for email #3 about Lesia Farmer — mine is only getting 22% opens."
8
Deal Closer — Handle Any Objection Confidently
Read before every sales call — takes 2 minutes
🏆
1
Open the objection you are most likely to hear today
There are 8 objection cards. Tap any card header to expand the full script below it. Read the script once before your call. You do not need to memorize it — just reading it puts the core argument in your head so when you hear the objection live, the response flows naturally.
2
Use the action buttons inside each objection card
Each expanded objection has 2–3 action buttons — Send case study, Book follow-up, Add to nurture, etc. Tap whichever matches what you decided to do after hearing the objection on a call. Each button fires a confirmation toast so you know the action registered.
3
Use the Closing Scripts at the bottom to close the deal
Scroll past the objections to find three closing scripts: Assumptive Close (use when they have said yes to everything — just walk them through checkout), Summary Close (recap everything they get, then ask if they are ready), and Urgency Close (use near a price increase or deadline). Tap Copy Script to copy any script to your clipboard.
🏆 The Assumptive Close is the most powerful — use it as your default
9
Customer Hub — Take Care of Every Buyer
A closed deal is not the end — it is the beginning
👥
1
Read each customer's journey badges
Every customer card shows a row of journey stage badges — completed stages are green, the current active stage is gold, and upcoming stages are grey. This tells you at a glance exactly where each buyer is in their workbook journey without asking them.
2
Tap Advance to move a customer to their next stage
When a customer completes a milestone — they finish Phase 1, they book their coaching call, they tell you they've started outreach — tap → Advance on their card. Their active badge shifts forward one stage and saves automatically. This keeps their journey current without any manual note-taking.
3
Use Check In to send a mid-journey touchpoint
Tap ✉ Check In on any customer card to trigger a check-in email notification. Use this when a customer has been quiet for more than 2 weeks — a simple "How is Assignment 7 going?" can re-engage them and open the door to an upsell conversation.
4
Use Upsell when a customer completes Phase 1
The best moment to offer a coaching upgrade ($197) or Full Suite ($497) is right after a customer finishes Phase 1 — Assignments 1 through 6. They have momentum, they have learned what the system can do, and they are ready for more. Tap ⬆ Upsell on their card and follow up with a personal message.
💰 Phase 1 completion is your best upsell trigger — act on it within 48 hours
10
Analytics — Your Weekly Business Review
Check once a week — not every day
📈
1
Check the four stat cards first
Total Revenue, Email List size, Average Open Rate, and Customer Lifetime Value. These four numbers tell you the health of your business at a glance. Revenue and list size should both be going up every week. If open rate drops below 30%, your subject lines need work.
2
Read the monthly revenue bar chart
The bars should trend upward month over month. Hover over any bar to see the exact revenue for that month. If you see a flat or down month, go to the AI Agent and ask: "What should I do differently this month to increase workbook sales?"
3
Find your biggest conversion drop-off in the funnel metrics
The Funnel Conversion Rates card shows 5 conversion percentages from Lead to Close. Find the one with the lowest percentage — that is your weakest link. Low email open rate? Fix your subject lines. Low click rate? Improve your email body copy. Low call-to-proposal rate? Ask the AI Agent to help you improve your discovery call structure.
🎯 Fix your weakest funnel stage first — that is always where the most money is hiding
4
Use the 12-month projection as your north star
The three projection boxes at the bottom show 30-day, 90-day, and 12-month revenue estimates based on your current growth rate. Look at the 12-month number once a week — not as pressure, but as proof that what you are doing is compounding. Stay consistent and that number becomes real.
